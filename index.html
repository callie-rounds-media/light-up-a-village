Around 1,000 homes in Cape Town get their own solar lights, and on the final night the whole village switches on together.

Life after dark

Thousands of homes
still run on candles.

FiresCandles and paraffin are one of the main causes of shack fires, which can burn through whole rows of homes in a single night.
SafetyWithout light, walking to the communal toilets after dark isn't safe, especially for women and girls.
R1,100That's roughly what a family spends on candles and paraffin every year, money that could go back into food.
SchoolWith light at home, kids can keep studying after the sun goes down.
A first for South Africa

Every home
in one village.

As far as we can find, no one has ever given every home in an entire settlement its own solar lights at once.
